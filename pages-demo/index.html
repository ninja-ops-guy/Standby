Marketplace state machine

How a transfer works

01

List

The seller publishes a prepaid booking with its face value, asking price, date, and transfer details.

02

Hold

A buyer claims the listing. Funds move into an escrow hold rather than being counted as completed revenue.

03

Transfer

The booking changes hands using the provider-supported handoff method or transfer code.

04

Settle

Once complete, one sale event records GMV, the seller net, and the platform fee.

Unit economics

Simple fee model, explicit ledger states.

The product prototype charges a 12% platform fee on completed transfers and optionally supports a $1.99 placement boost. The real application repository includes transactional claim, cancellation, refund, payout, and settlement paths backed by PostgreSQL.
